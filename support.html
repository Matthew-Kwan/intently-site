Support

Intently shows you your own words before an app you open by reflex appears. You continue on purpose, or you leave. Nothing is ever blocked.

Getting started

  1. Write this week's intent. What you'd rather be doing: reading before bed, an early run, sleep.
  2. Allow Screen Time access and choose your apps. Apple's picker keeps your choices private; Intently never sees which apps they are.
  3. Write a few words for each app. Why you're stepping back from it, and what you hope to gain. These are the words you'll see when you open it.

Questions

Does Intently block apps?
No. When you open a chosen app you see a check-in card with your own words and two choices: Continue, for the number of minutes you set (1, 5 or 15), or Leave. After those minutes of use the card comes back and asks again. You can always continue.
Why does the card show the same words every time?
On purpose. The reflex is what the card interrupts, and repetition is what wears a reflex down. The count on the card ("Continued 3 times today · 18 min") is what changes.
How do I change what the card says?
Tap an app on the home screen to rewrite its words, or tap Edit intent to rewrite the week's intent. Intently nudges you to rewrite it every Sunday.
What is the daily resolve?
The number of continued minutes you'd like to stay under each day. Days under it count toward your streak.
Can Intently see which apps I chose or how I use them?
No. Apple's Screen Time framework hands Intently anonymous tokens and only tells it when the minutes you allowed have been used. Nothing leaves your phone.
Why does iOS ask me to allow Screen Time access?
That permission is what lets Intently show the check-in card over other apps. It applies only to your device and you can withdraw it any time in Settings → Screen Time.
The card doesn't appear.
Check that Screen Time access is allowed for Intently (Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access) and that the app is still in your chosen list. Restarting the iPhone resolves the rare case where iOS stops showing shields.
What's the free trial, and what does the unlock cost?
Everything is free for 30 days. After that, the Full Unlock is a single purchase of US$2.99 (or the equivalent in your currency). There is no subscription. If you don't unlock, check-ins stop and your apps open normally.
How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?
Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchases on Intently's home screen or on the unlock screen.
What does Intently require?
An iPhone running iOS 17 or later. No account and no internet connection.
