Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Intently doesn't collect your data.

Intently is an independent iPhone app that shows you your own words before an app you chose opens. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party software kits, and it makes no network connections of its own. Everything you write and every check-in stays on your phone.

What Intently accesses on your device

Screen Time

Intently uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to show a check-in card over the apps you pick and to notice when the minutes you allowed have been used. You choose those apps through Apple's own picker, and Apple deliberately hides your choices from us: Intently receives only anonymous tokens and never learns which apps you selected. Your authorization applies to your own device only.

Local storage

Your weekly intent, the words you write for each app, your settings and your daily counts are stored in Intently's app storage on your device, shared only with Intently's own extensions that draw the card. Deleting the app removes all of it.

What we don't collect

We do not collect, store or transmit any personal information: not your name, email, location, contacts, which apps you use, how long you use them, or anything you write. Intently has no servers. In App Store privacy terms, Intently's answer is Data Not Collected.

Purchases

Intently offers a free 30-day Trial and a one-time Full Unlock. Both are in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple through the App Store under Apple's privacy policy. Intently only learns whether a purchase or trial is active, through Apple's StoreKit, so it can keep working after the free month. We never receive your payment information.

Children

Intently is not directed at children, and because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If Intently ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
